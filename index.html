How It Works

OUR
ORBIT CYCLE

01

DISCOVERY

Understand the business, audience and problem. We deep-dive into your goals, audience expectations and competitive landscape.

  • Goal & audience definition
  • Competitor landscape analysis
  • Scope, budget & timeline mapping
  • Brand & tone alignment workshop
02

DIRECTION

Define the strategy, positioning and experience. We set the strategic foundation and creative trajectory that will make your business stand out.

  • Experience architecture & user flows
  • Content strategy & messaging hierarchy
  • Visual moodboarding & creative direction
  • Key interaction principles
03

DESIGN

Turn the direction into a strong visual system and interface. Every pixel is intentional. Moodboards, wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes before a single line of code.

  • Moodboard & style exploration
  • Wireframes & user flows
  • High-fidelity UI in Figma
  • Interactive prototype review
04

BUILD

Develop the experience into a responsive website/product. Clean, scalable code with performance, accessibility and fluid interactions at the core.

  • Frontend & modern web development
  • Interactive micro-animations & transitions
  • QA, cross-browser & mobile testing
  • Performance, speed & SEO optimization
05

IMPROVE

Launch, learn and refine. Deploy, monitor and support. Going live is not the end — it's the beginning of the orbit.

  • Deployment & domain setup
  • SEO & analytics configuration
  • Post-launch performance monitoring
  • Ongoing retainer support available

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What services does Axilyn Studio offer?

We offer website design and development, social media management, SEO and local search optimisation, branding and visual design, and website maintenance — everything your business needs to grow online.

Does Axilyn Studio work with businesses outside Bangalore?

Absolutely. While we are a digital studio based in Bangalore, we work with startups, businesses and brands across India and worldwide. All collaborations are handled remotely with clear communication.

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical business website takes 2–4 weeks. E-commerce websites and custom web applications may take 4–8 weeks depending on scope and complexity.

Do you offer SEO services in Bangalore?

Yes. We provide on-page SEO, technical SEO, local SEO, Google Business Profile optimisation, and keyword strategy for businesses in Bangalore and across India.

What is the cost of website development at Axilyn Studio?

Our pricing depends on project scope, features and timeline. Contact us via the form above for a free consultation and custom quote tailored to your business.
