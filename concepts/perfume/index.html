THE IDEA

TRANSLATING INVISIBLE NOTES
INTO SENSORY DIGITAL LIGHT.

Buying niche luxury perfume online is traditionally a leap of faith. Without smelling the fragrance, customers need visual metaphors that convey top notes, heart transitions and dry-down longevity.

This concept translates the olfactory pyramid into interactive light, evaporation curves and a curated 5-vial discovery set that converts curiosity into confidence.

THE PROBLEM

FRAGRANCE E-COMMERCE
RELIES TOO HEAVILY
ON GUESSWORK.

01

INVISIBLE INGREDIENTS

Listing 'amber, fig, bergamot' does not explain how a fragrance actually blooms on skin.

02

HIGH BLIND-BUY RISK

A $280 bottle purchase with zero prior tactile sensation causes immense customer drop-off.

03

STERILE CATALOGS

Standard e-commerce grids fail to build the atmospheric world necessary for niche luxury perfume.

DESIGN DIRECTION

ETHIC.
LUMINOUS.
OLFACTORY.

The design bridges the physical weight of heavy glass flacons with ethereal light trails, creating a digital experience as transportive as the scent itself.

PRINCIPLE

ATMOSPHERIC REVEAL

Fluid gradients and warm amber glow evoke resinous dry-downs and fresh citrus top notes.

PRINCIPLE

TACTILE COMMERCE

Every interactive flacon view accentuates the heavy bevelled glass and textured leather caps.

PRINCIPLE

FRICTION-FREE SAMPLING

Lowers barrier to entry by crediting 100% of sample kit cost toward any full-size purchase.
