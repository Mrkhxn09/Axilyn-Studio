SPACES,

Most architectural websites present spaces as static image galleries. This concept explores how digital pacing, materiality and editorial storytelling can make interior architecture feel as sensory online as it does in the physical world.

THE IDEA

LET CRAFTSMANSHIP BREATHE
BEFORE THE FIRST MEETING.

High-end architectural clients don't choose an architect from a crowded grid of small thumbnail images. They evaluate taste, spatial atmosphere, material restraint and intentionality.

This concept uses asymmetric editorial pacing, macro material breakdowns and private commission questionnaires to create a digital salon experience rather than an impersonal portfolio.

THE PROBLEM

PORTFOLIOS OFTEN
FLAT-LINE THE EMOTION
OF PHYSICAL SPACES.

01

DILUTED SCALE

Small thumbnail grids strip monolithic architecture of its true physical weight and volume.

02

MISSING MATERIALITY

Without texture and lighting context, high-end craftsmanship looks like generic stock interiors.

03

TRANSACTIONAL INQUIRIES

Standard web forms feel out of place for bespoke, multi-million architectural commissions.

DESIGN DIRECTION

TACTILE.
EDITORIAL.
MONOLITHIC.

Drawing cues from luxury architectural monographs, the interface balances raw concrete tones, warm Hinoki cypress accents and serene typographic pacing that respects the work.

PRINCIPLE

MONUMENTAL SPACING

Generous negative space echoes the quiet grandeur of private residences.

PRINCIPLE

MATERIAL REVEAL

Every material specification is given the scrutiny of an art monograph.

PRINCIPLE

PRIVATE DIALOGUE

The commission inquiry feels like an invitation into a private design atelier.
